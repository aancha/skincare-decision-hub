# Residual experiment data card

## Evidence design

The final evaluation used 95 fresh pairwise judgments from owner-reviewed slates, separated from development contexts and frozen before final evaluation.

## Limitations

- One owner/reviewer limits generalizability.
- The supported slice excluded broad browsing and safety-sensitive contexts.
- Coverage of the deterministic top five was incomplete.
- Product identifiers and raw catalog snapshots are retailer-derived and are not included here.

## Public-showcase substitution

The runnable showcase uses 12 fictional products. It demonstrates UI and deterministic safety behavior; it does not reproduce the private model evaluation dataset.
